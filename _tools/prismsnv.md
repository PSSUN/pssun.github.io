description: >-
  PrismSNV is a single-cell modeling framework that redefines single nucleotide variants (SNVs) as endogenous perturbations of cellular state. It measures the functional impact of SNVs directly at the single-cell level by quantifying mutation-induced transcriptomic displacement, capturing acute cell-autonomous effects rather than signatures shaped by long-term selection, and uncovering how SNVs shift their roles across stages of tumor evolution.
